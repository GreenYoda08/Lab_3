The timer in the upper corner of the HUD ticked down to thirty seconds as the overtime alarm blared through our headsets. 
The cart was stuck three meters from the final checkpoint on Yggsgard, pinned under a barrage of laser fire from an 
opposing Hela who had been dominating the lobby all match.

"Magneto, pull up that Great Shield now!" I shouted into comms, adjusting my grip on the controller.

Playing Luna Snow on a push map is non-stop stress, but this was the final stretch. My teammate popped Magneto’s 
shield right on the payload, granting us just enough breathing room to contest. I cast Absolute Zero right into the 
crowd, freezing their Venom mid-dive before he could wipe our backline.

"I’m launching the Ultimate!" our Spider-Man yelled.

He swung over the archway, diving straight into the fight with Get Over Here! to yank Hela off her high-ground perch. 
The enemy team scrambled, their formation shattering as our Iron Man activated his team-up bonus, raining down 
enhanced repulsor blasts from above.

I activated Fate of the Both, surging forward while alternating between healing beams for our dying Magneto and ice shards 
into the enemy Magik. The kill feed lit up with bright green text. Magneto executed a heavy knockback, clearing the 
last defender off the objective.

"Just push it! Stay on the cart!"

The meter crawled forward: two meters, one meter, zero.

The bright orange victory banner slammed across the screen just as my health dropped into the red. We dropped our controllers 
and let out a collective breath, high-fiving in the voice channel. After eighteen minutes of absolute chaos, clutch ultimate abilities, and endless team fights, we had finally broken their defense to claim the win.
